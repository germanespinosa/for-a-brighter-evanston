A broader public voice

Thank you for supporting the District 65 poll.

The polling fundraiser is now closed. Thank you to everyone who contributed and helped spread the word.

Our kids deserve stability. Our community deserves a voice.

Our community deserves a meaningful voice in the future of its schools. We are grateful to the neighbors who contributed to this professional polling effort and shared it with others. The poll is intended to help us better understand the views and priorities of registered voters across Evanston/Skokie District 65.

What the poll explores

  • Trust. How confident are voters in the board and administration, their financial management, and whether community input is being heard?
  • The budget plan. Do voters support the current proposal, or would they rather delay closures until there's a fuller financial and academic plan?
  • A future referendum. Would voters support one, and would closing Dewey and Lincolnwood make them more or less likely to?
  • Priorities. Academic achievement, closing achievement gaps, neighborhood schools, balanced budgets, property taxes.
  • Rebuilding support. What would earn a yes vote: spending limits, citizen oversight, protecting neighborhood schools, building improvements?

One piece of a bigger effort

Neighbors are examining this proposal from every angle. A legal effort is asking whether the district has met its obligations.[2] Financial reviewers, including the Legion of Data Nerds, are checking the numbers, and the reported $17 million forecasting error shows why that matters.[1][3]

The poll adds what's still missing: what voters support, what they oppose, and what might rebuild their trust. Whatever happens with the current proposal, that evidence helps the board, the city and residents build a plan that lasts, so families don't face the same school closure battle year after year. A poll can't guarantee that. It can help us plan past the immediate crisis.

What happens next

We will share all findings, the full questionnaire, the methodology and financial records as soon as they become available.

We want everyone to be able to understand what the results do and don’t tell us, and how contributions are used. Thank you for helping bring a broader public voice into decisions about our schools.
