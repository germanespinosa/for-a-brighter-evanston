For a Brighter Evanston

Privacy

This page explains what happens to the information you give us when you take the referendum pledge.

What we collect

Your first name, last name and email, whether you checked the box for campaign updates, the language you used, the website where you signed, and the date and time.

How we use it

We use it to count each person once and to show the District 65 board how many neighbors have pledged. Only the total number is shown publicly. We never show or share names or emails, including with the websites that display the pledge.

Campaign updates

Signing the pledge does not add you to our email list. We add you only if you check the updates box. Every email we send includes a link to unsubscribe.

Where it is kept

In a Google Sheet that only the For a Brighter Evanston campaign team can open, and in our Mailchimp email list if you asked for updates.

Questions or changes

To remove your pledge or ask a question, email {{ site.links.email }}.
